# IM3180: Design and Innovation Project (DIP)
For the academic year AY26/27, Semester 1.

## Background
This project was developed by a group of six students, with the goal to develop a "fun and engaging" game. Development spanned the course of 13 weeks, with a presentation carried out on the final week of the semester. 

_**This repository is made public for the purpose of grading, after which it will be made private**_.

## Outline
**State.Flow** features a blend of rhythm and tower-defense genres, incorporating their elements into a new experience that gives both genres equal attention and uptime. 
It was developed in Unity Engine (C#), with level data stored as XML files. Assets were hand-drawn by several of my team members in Piskel. 

The game features three levels, with the player's objective being to destroy the bosses featured in each level and to survive the waves of minions that spawn periodically by placing automatic turrets and defending against boss arrow attacks.
